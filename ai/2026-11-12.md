## Strategic analysis

**Industry direction: the window is the start of a governance-led rotation, not a capability-led one.** The prior-briefing read ("where does Anthropic strike?") still stands as the operative frontier-lab signal through Wed Nov-18. But the in-window window inserts a second, harder question: *does Nvidia still have Jensen Huang?* These are genuinely independent events, both resolve by Mon Nov-17 → Wed Nov-18, and together they calibrate every AI-infrastructure commitment made between now and the end of November. A base case where Huang stays (board declines to remove him, Nov-17 earnings print clean, Anthropic prices in-range) is a continuation of the Oct–Nov pattern.

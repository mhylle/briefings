- **A second skeptical angle: the Congressional Dec-01 closed-door interview may contain the actually-market-moving information, not the Nov-12 announcement itself.** The Nov-12 8-K documents a resignation; the Dec-01 interview could document the predicate — whether the Alice Huang relationship involved intelligence targeting, what internal-control weaknesses the investigation surfaced, and whether any Nvidia-internal data or roadmap information reached China-linked parties. A material disclosure on Dec-01 would reintroduce overhang into the stock, re-weight the competitive-silicon reprice case, and shift sovereign-procurement case strength materially. The in-window Nov-12 primary resolves the leadership question; it does not resolve the predicate.
